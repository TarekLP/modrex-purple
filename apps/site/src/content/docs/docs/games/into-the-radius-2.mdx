Into the Radius 2 support covers SML (S.M.A.R.T. Mod Loader) mods, standard Unreal pak mods, logic mods, and UE4SS Lua mods.

<GameFacts game="itr2" />

## Mod source

Into the Radius 2 uses the Nexus Mods catalog. ModWorkshop is not available for ITR2 in Modrex, so Browse always shows Nexus Mods.

## Install targets

Modrex routes each archive to the folder the game's loader reads, based on the archive's contents:

- **SML mods** — a `.uplugin` sharing its base name with a `.pak`/`.ucas`/`.utoc` goes into `IntoTheRadius2/Content/Mods`.
- **Standard paks** — pak mods go into `IntoTheRadius2/Content/Paks/Mods`.
- **Logic mods** — a pak under a `LogicMods/` segment goes into `IntoTheRadius2/Content/Paks/LogicMods`.
- **Lua mods** — UE4SS Lua mods (`enabled.txt` / `Scripts/main.lua`) go into `IntoTheRadius2/Content/Paks/LuaMods`.

## Priority

All four ITR2 targets are directory-based and are not renamed for priority. Follow the loader's and mod author's conflict guidance when two mods change the same content.
